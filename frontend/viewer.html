    MISSION OVERVIEW

    SPHEREx mission

    SPHEREx (Spectro-Photometer for the History of the Universe, Epoch of Reionization and Ices Explorer) launched on March 11, 2025. Its planned two-year all-sky spectral survey will observe more than 450 million galaxies and 100 million Milky Way stars to investigate the origins of the universe.
