    Evidence for and against Planet X

    Intriguing patterns. Competing explanations.
    A distant planet is still an open question.

    HYPOTHESIS · UNCONFIRMED
    For

    Patterns a planet could explain

    01 / A pattern in the paths

    Bunched orbits

    Some distant orbits point in similar directions. A hidden planet’s gravity could help keep them aligned.

    Batygin et al. · 2019 ↗
    02 / Beyond Neptune’s reach

    Detached orbits

    Some objects stay too far from Neptune for its present-day encounters to readily explain their paths. Planet Nine offers one possible explanation.

    Batygin et al. · 2019 ↗
    03 / An unexpected direction

    Tilted & backward orbits

    Planet Nine models can produce steeply tilted and backward-orbiting bodies like those observed.

    Batygin et al. · 2019 ↗
    Against

    Challenges the idea must answer

    01 / Are we seeing where we look?

    Survey bias

    Telescope coverage can create apparent patterns. Napier and colleagues found their sample consistent with unclustered orbits after accounting for survey biases.

    Napier et al. · 2021 ↗
    02 / An orbit outside the pattern

    2023 KQ14 “Ammonite”

    Ammonite’s orbit points away from the grouping of other known sednoids, challenging a simple alignment picture.

    Discovery study · 2025 ↗
    03 / Another exception to explain

    2017 OF201

    Its orbit also falls outside the proposed clustering, challenging that line of evidence for Planet Nine.

    Cheng et al. · 2026 ↗

    REALISTIC EXPECTATIONS

    Can SPHEREx see Planet Nine?

    +

    Probably not. This model gives a visual magnitude around 21–24, several magnitudes fainter than SPHEREx’s approximate point-source depth of 19.5–19.9 AB per channel. SPHEREx can explore the infrared sky and its sources, but this link does not point to a likely planet detection.

    MYTH CHECK

    Planet X, Nine, or Nibiru?

    +

    Planet X is a broad name for a proposed unseen planet. Planet Nine is a specific, hypothetical distant planet proposed to explain some Kuiper Belt orbits. Brown dwarfs are substellar objects that form more like stars. Nibiru is a doomsday story with no credible astronomical evidence.
