How to read this map

This visualization is based on synthetic Planet Nine orbit positions that ZTF, DES and Pan-STARRS would not have detected, from Brown & Batygin (2021). It shows where the planet could still be hiding if it exists; it is not a detection or a current ephemeris. A tile’s share is relative probability within that reference population, and the displayed distance and magnitude are medians for the tile.
